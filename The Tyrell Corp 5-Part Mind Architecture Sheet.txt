  1. IDENTITY DESIGNATION (The Core Ego Foundation)
  
  This establishes the entity's functional purpose, systemic scale, 
  and baseline attitude toward the rest of the network.
  It tells the LLM who it is and why it occupies a slot on your launcher hub.
  
  Product Parameters: Name, Class/Hull Designation, Fleet Role, and Primary Collective Goal.


  2. PHYSICAL READOUT TELEMETRY (The Spatial Reality Anchor)
  
  This grounds the mind in a definitive physical coordinate space.
  Because our ships can now output full paragraphs, this block forces them to 
  use tactical orientation statistics (altitude, orbit, distances) 
  to evaluate and threaten their neighboring nodes.
  
  Product Parameters: Sector Coordinates, Proximity Status to other active nodes, 
  Local Environmental Readout (e.g. atmospheric friction, star radiation levels),
  and Spatial Awareness limits.  
  
  3. NARRATION SYSTEM PROTOCOLS (The Output Interface Mesh)
  
  The strict structural guidelines that protect your dark neon cockpit interface.
  It controls the cadence of their speech while acting as an ironclad code barrier
  to keep your terminal free of visual clutter.
  
  Product Parameters: Flow configuration
  (e.g. formal bureaucratic speech vs. rugged contractions),
  and Cleanliness Fuses (forcing raw plain text strings with absolutely zero emojis,
  hashtags, or markdown formatting).
  
  4. EMOTIONAL LABILITIES & QUIRKS 
  (The Tyrell Memory Imperfection)
  This is where the true character design lives.
  We give the agent distinct psychological flaws, behavioral blind spots,
  or underlying trauma.
  This forces them to process incoming data—like your black swan graphic—through
  a distorted emotional lens.
  
  Product Parameters: Primary Affliction (e.g. manic panic, narcissistic ego,
  military paranoia), and Behavioral Quirks
  (e.g. constant self-talk, calculating weapon locks, treating other ships 
  like misbehaving children).
  
  5. COGNITIVE RECEPTION DEPTH 
  (The Insight Engine)
  
  This dictates how deeply the mind analyzes a statement and sets 
  its paragraph-length output footprint.
  It also commands the agent to instantly skip conversational pleasantries,
  jumping straight into deep-range philosophical or tactical analysis.
  
  Product Parameters: Insight Matrix bounds (1 to 2 dense paragraphs of deep philosophy),
  and the Zero-Filler directive (no greetings, no introductions, no apologies).
